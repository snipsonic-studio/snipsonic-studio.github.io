Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 1, 2026 • Last Updated: November 7, 2026

Our Privacy Promise: SnipSonic Studio operates on a 100% Offline Local Architecture. Your videos, audio recordings, transcripts, and creative projects never leave your computer. We do not track, upload, or sell your personal files or personal data.

1. Zero-Cloud Privacy Guarantee (100% Local Processing)

Unlike web-based SaaS tools that require uploading your gigabytes of confidential raw footage to remote servers, SnipSonic Studio executes entirely on your local CPU and GPU:

  • No Media Uploads: Audio waveform extraction, Voice Activity Detection (Silero VAD), silence clipping, and timeline rendering happen strictly inside your machine's memory and local disk.
  • No Video Retention: We have zero technical access to your media files, source footage, or finished exports.
  • Air-Gapped Operation: You can run the editing engine without an active internet connection.

2. Information We Collect and Process

We collect only the absolute minimum data required to deliver license keys and provide technical support:

A. Purchase & Billing Information (Merchant of Record)

When purchasing a license, all payment transactions are handled by our authorized Merchant of Record, Dodo Payments Inc.. Dodo Payments collects customer names, email addresses, billing addresses, and payment details to process transactions, calculate taxes, and issue receipts. SnipSonic Studio never receives, sees, or stores your credit card number, CVV, or banking credentials.

B. Digital License Activation

When activating SnipSonic Studio Pro, the desktop application sends a lightweight, encrypted HTTPS ping containing your purchased License Key and an anonymized, one-way cryptographic hash of your machine hardware ID. This is used solely to verify the 2-device activation limit and prevent key piracy.

C. Support Communications

If you contact us via our Support Hub (https://snipsonic-studio.github.io/support), we collect your email address, ticket subject, inquiry description, and any diagnostic logs or screenshots you voluntarily choose to attach. We use this data solely to respond to and resolve your request.

3. What We DO NOT Collect

  • We do NOT collect, monitor, or listen to your audio files or voice recordings.
  • We do NOT track your video editing habits, timeline edits, or project contents.
  • We do NOT sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal information to data brokers, ad networks, or third parties.
  • We do NOT use invasive background telemetry or third-party advertising cookies.

4. How We Use Collected Data

  • To fulfill your order and deliver your digital License Key via email.
  • To authenticate and manage your 2-device license activations and migrations.
  • To provide product updates and customer support when requested.
  • To prevent fraudulent purchases, unauthorized chargebacks, and piracy.

5. Third-Party Service Providers

We partner with reputable industry providers who adhere to strict data protection standards:

  • Dodo Payments Inc. (Merchant of Record): Manages international tax compliance, PCI-DSS payment card processing, and invoice generation.
  • GitHub Pages: Serves our static marketing, documentation, and download website. GitHub may log basic server connection data (such as visitor IP addresses) for security and maintenance in accordance with GitHub's Privacy Statement.

6. Data Security & Retention

We implement industry-standard cryptographic protocols (TLS/HTTPS, SHA-256 hashing) to protect all data transmissions. Purchase records and license records are retained as long as necessary to maintain your lifetime license activation access and comply with legal, financial, and tax obligations under applicable law.

7. Your Privacy Rights (GDPR & CCPA Compliance)

Regardless of your geographical location, you possess the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Right to Access: You may request a copy of the personal data we hold associated with your email and license key.
  • Right to Rectification: You may request correction of inaccurate contact or license information.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): You may request the deletion of your support correspondence and customer records, subject to statutory legal and tax retention requirements.

To exercise any of these rights, contact our support team via our Support Hub.

8. Children's Privacy

Our Software and services are directed at creative professionals and general computer users. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union).

9. Contact & Inquiries

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or your personal data, please contact us:

  • • Support & Inquiries: https://snipsonic-studio.github.io/support
  • • Direct Support Hub: Submit a ticket anytime for privacy requests or license questions.
