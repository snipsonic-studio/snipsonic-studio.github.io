Frequently asked questions

Does SnipSonic upload media to external servers?

No. Every audio waveform calculation, voice activity scan, and Whisper transcription runs entirely inside your machine using local CPU and GPU instructions. Your raw media never leaves your disk.

Which NLE video editors are supported?

SnipSonic exports native timeline project files for Adobe Premiere Pro (.xml), DaVinci Resolve (.fcpxml), and Apple Final Cut Pro (.fcpxml). You can continue grading and color editing seamlessly.

How do license activations work?

Each lifetime license key allows activation on two computers simultaneously (for example, your main editing workstation and a laptop). You can deactivate and transfer seats at any time.

What video containers and audio formats work?

All standard containers: MP4, MOV, MKV, WebM, ProRes, MP3, WAV, AAC, and FLAC, powered by an integrated high-performance FFmpeg media pipeline.
