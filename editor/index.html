Frequently Asked Questions

Is SnipSonic Web Editor really free?

Yes, SnipSonic Web Studio is 100% free with no watermark, no sign-up wall, and no credit card required. Export trimmed MP4 video and 16-bit WAV audio directly from your browser.

Does SnipSonic upload my video to any server?

No. SnipSonic runs 100% client-side in your web browser using Web Audio API and FFmpeg WebAssembly. Your raw footage never leaves your device.

How does Smart Zoom (1.0x-1.3x) improve video retention?

Per-clip Smart Zoom alternates between 1.0x wide shots and subtle 1.15x to 1.3x punch-ins centered on the speaker across speech cuts, boosting retention on short-form and tutorial videos.

What voice hesitations does the Web Editor detect?

Basic Voice Hesitations detects common sustained hesitation sounds (uh, um, ah, er) inside spoken segments and displays them on a dedicated timeline lane for one-click review.
